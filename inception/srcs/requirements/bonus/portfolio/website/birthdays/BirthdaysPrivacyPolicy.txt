Privacy Policy for "Birthdays" App
Effective Date: 11/12/2024

1. Information Collection
Our app only collects the birthdate entered by the user to calculate the user's age. No other personal data is collected or stored.

2. Data Usage
All calculations are performed locally on the device. No data is sent to external servers or shared with third-party services.

3. Third-Party Services
Our app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or other services that collect user data.

4. Data Retention
No personal data is stored beyond the current session. The app does not store or retain your birthdate once the session is ended.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected here.
